## Anti-Patterns

- **Latest explicit request**: Add a results post each day using GA4/GSC for sites Nathan has worked on. Named examples: IR Energy, M&J Roofing, DS Carpentry, K Williams Roofing and NC Digital. Partial SEO support for the client sites; NC Digital's site started around a year ago in a competitive niche.
- **Topics to avoid**: Unsupported results, invented attribution, and generic project announcements presented as fresh launches.
- Only Davies Property, IR Energy, M&J Roofing, DS Carpentry and K Williams Roofing may be described as receiving partial SEO support. For every other portfolio results post, use the wording "We built and host the website" and do not imply NC Digital carried out SEO for that client.
- Do not add analytics source footers such as "Source: GA4, all countries" to social posts. Present modest figures as factual website snapshots rather than impressive SEO wins.
- Do not use declining metrics, weaker-period comparisons or language about losses in promotional social posts. Select accurate positive figures or a neutral current-period snapshot that presents NC Digital's work well.
- NC Digital does not host Milne Industrial or IR Energy. For Milne Industrial, say that Milne supplied the design and NC Digital developed the website. Do not make a hosting claim for either business.
- Do not feature very new sites in results posts unless their verified early figures are clearly interesting or impressive. Electrician Barry is excluded from results posts.
- Results posts about websites NC Digital built should include the featured website URL and finish with a direct invitation such as “Want a website like this? Get in touch today.” Keep the NC Digital service URL as the final destination link.
- **Tones to avoid**: Hype, generic expert lecturing, engagement bait and false urgency.
- **Claims**: No invented client results, testimonials, statistics, rankings or personal experiences. Verify offers and prices before using them.
- **Access**: Do not include account tokens or credentials in context files, captions or reports.

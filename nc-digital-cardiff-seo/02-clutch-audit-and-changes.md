## Can NC Digital appear on Clutch's Cardiff list?
clutch.co/web-designers/uk/wales/cardiff ranks #3 on Google for "web design cardiff". Clutch builds its city lists from office locations, so NC Digital won't show there without a Cardiff office. Inventing one is a misrepresentation and against Clutch's rules. More realistic options:
- Clutch also has a **Wales list** and location filters that count service focus and reviews, so fixing the service lines and getting reviews helps there.
- In the description and portfolio, say that you serve Cardiff and describe the work you've done for nearby clients (Barry, Caerphilly).

## About NC Digital
Small businesses in South Wales often get two kinds of quote for a website: a cheap template that's hard to change and doesn't show up on Google, or an agency price they can't justify. NC Digital sits between the two.

Based in Merthyr Tydfil and working with businesses across Cardiff, Newport, Caerphilly, Swansea and the valleys, NC Digital designs and builds WordPress websites that are fast, mobile-friendly and set up for search from day one. A one-page website starts from £199, larger multi-page builds are quoted around your brief, and ecommerce sites using WooCommerce, Shopify or SureCart start from £800.

New businesses that would rather not pay upfront can choose a managed starter website from £20 a month including VAT. That covers the domain, hosting and ongoing management.

Every project is handled by founder Nathan Constance, a professional web developer since 2015 who built his first website in 2008. You speak to the person building your site, and you keep full access to update it yourself afterwards.

Alongside new builds, NC Digital provides:
- Local SEO and Google Business Profile setup
- UK hosting with SSL and security monitoring
- Website maintenance and updates
- Business email setup
- Logo and brand identity

Clients include roofers, plumbers, electricians, property developers and renewable energy installers across South Wales.
